How to use AR

Hold your phone so the back camera sees your hand. Videos float in a 3D ring around you and stay in place as you turn.

☝
Point with your index fingerA reticle follows your fingertip. The video you point at lights up.
👌
Quick pinch on a videoOpens it. Quick pinch again to go back.
↔
Pinch, hold and dragGrab the ring and spin it. Let go while moving to throw it.
⤢
Two hands, pinch and pull apartStretch the space bigger, or push together to shrink it.
