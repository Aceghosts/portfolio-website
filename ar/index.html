How to use AR

Hold your phone so the back camera sees your hand. Videos float in a 3D ring around you and stay in place as you turn.

↔
Pinch, then swipe left or rightTurn the ring to the next or previous video.
↕
Pinch, then swipe up or downTilt the ring to see it from above or below.
👌
Quick pinchOpen the video at the front. Quick pinch again to go back.
⤢
Thumb and index, open and closeSpread them apart to move into the space, bring them together to move out.
